--- RELATIONSHIP RULES ---

HASONE means:
Parent.hasOne(Child)
Child.belongsTo(Parent)

HASMANY means:
Parent.hasMany(Child)
Child.belongsTo(Parent)

Fields like name, content, key, value, data, success, error, reason, shouldTerminate are model columns, not relationships.

Use camelCase foreign keys:
AgentConfig -> agentConfigId
ToolRegistry -> toolRegistryId
AgentStep -> agentStepId

Use plural aliases for HASMANY.
Use singular aliases for HASONE.
